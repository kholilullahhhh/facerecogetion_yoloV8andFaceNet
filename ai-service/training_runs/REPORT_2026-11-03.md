## 2. FaceNet — Embedding Identitas (model produksi)

| Konfigurasi | Nilai |
|---|---|
| Basis | InceptionResnetV1 (VGGFace2) |
| Fine-tune | hanya `block8` + `last_bn` + head Dropout(0.2)→Linear(512→9) — 1.6M params trainable |
| Data | 9 siswa terdaftar (001–010 tanpa 008): 75 train / 18 val (2 foto val per siswa) |
| Augmentasi | rotasi ±10°, brightness ×1.15/×0.85, flip H — mirror pipeline produksi |
| Optimizer | AdamW, LR backbone 5e-5 / head 1e-3, wd 1e-4, label smoothing 0.1 |
| Epoch | 30 · batch 16 · val acc = **1.000** (pertama tercapai epoch 7 → dipakai sebagai checkpoint best) |
